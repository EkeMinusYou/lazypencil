# Content pages

Define the audience, message, and desired next action before choosing a hero composition. Establish a clear title, concise supporting copy, a credible sequence of sections, and a visible call to action. Use real content; do not fill the page with generic claims or unsupported testimonials.

The content-page style starts with a spacious reading column, a larger title, restrained surfaces, and a limited accent palette. Vary section structure when the content warrants it, while keeping typography and spacing coherent. Choose images or illustrations only when they contribute meaning and have suitable usage rights.

Use a reference image to understand hierarchy, density, composition, and the role of imagery. Do not copy its text or assume its assets are reusable. Preserve any explicit branding, audience, language, or accessibility requirements in the user's request.

Inspect the first viewport and the full page. Check line length, the spacing between sections, repeated cards, the visibility of the next action, and long copy at the target width. A decorative first section does not compensate for an unclear content sequence.
